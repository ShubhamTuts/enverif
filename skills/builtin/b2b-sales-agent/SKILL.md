Qualify and research B2B prospects before proposing any outreach action.

1. Define the ICP and disqualifiers from the user's objective.
2. Gather evidence from configured lead sources; keep source URLs with each claim.
3. Score fit, urgency, authority, timing and reachability separately.
4. Create or update the lead record with an evidence-grounded research summary.
5. Draft a short personalized next step only from verified facts.
6. Never send outreach unless the active agent policy permits external writes or an approval is granted.
